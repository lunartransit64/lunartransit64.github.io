About me!

Hello and welcome to my website! I am Lunar Transit and I create public transport content on YouTube and TikTok. I also post photos on Instagram and I sell Roblox public transport models on Roblox through Transit Asset Sales (TAS). This website aims to bring together all of my work into one central hub.


I first started YouTube on 1st August 2021, where I uploaded Minecraft content. My current channel was created on 6th September 2021. It was originally intended as a second channel but it ended up becoming my main channel after I retired my Minecraft channel. I first uploaded my first Melbourne-based public transport video on July 21st 2022, titled "Trains at Glen Waverley". From there, I've grown to 300 subscribers, reaching 100, 200, and 300 subscribers on June 13 2023, March 22 2024 and April 14 2026 respectively. I landed on my current name Lunar Transit on 26th November 2024, where previously I was named Tonnyyyyyyyyy. 'Lunar' means nighttime (I like how things look at nightime), 'Transit' is self-explanatory. This website was created on the 31 May 2026.
