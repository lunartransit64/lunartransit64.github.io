The history of the modern station signage

Thuresday, 16 July 2026


2016

In 2016, some maps were updated with the new style. On the 28th December 2016, the new network map was revealed here

2017

Some of the first prototypes of the modern signage appeared at Flinders Street in late-2017 as part of the Flinders Street station update being prototype direction signage. From there, the design evolved in 2018 and 2019 to the style simular to today.


In July 2020, the first station (from what I know of) outside of Flinders Street received the new signage being Mentone station as apart of the Level Crossing Removal Project. From there, only new stations or stations getting upgrades received the new signage. The design partially changed in 2024 with the removal of the PTV logo, and then in 2025 with the addition of the Transport Victoria logo.


In June and July 2026, for the first time stations that were not receiving upgrades or are major interchanges received the new platform signage. This included Newport, Williamstown, Sandringham, Prahran and Werribee. More stations are set to receive new signage in the coming months and years
